Scene graph branch viewer

Each git ref played over the same captures, through its own adapter and engine. The robot moves along its recorded poses. For each capture the route is the planner's route from the robot's current pose to the chosen goal (five recorded places along the drive; the goal is always marked). The free space is coloured by clearance (or by the planner's price). Map, clearance, price and charts are all for the same capture as the robot. The checks are listed below.

clearance of free cells: dark red = 0.25 m (the robot touches), yellow = 0.8 m, pale = 2 m or more planner price (log scale, the run's maximum = dark red): white = no charge excluded: the 0.5 m square does not fit (hard) lethal (latest report: occupied) lethal held by a later close look (1.5 m) unknown, never reported route corridor to the goal (0.5 m); solid line = centre goal (always shown; green ring) robot footprint now (0.5 m square, white line = heading) chart anchors; blue lines = links between charts

Checks. start_snap: the route begins within 0.5 m of the robot. goal_snap: it ends within 0.5 m of the last pose. nodes_nav: every node is on a navigable cell. segments_nav: sampled points of every segment are navigable. length: the route is at least the straight-line distance between its ends. no_route, start_not_navigable and goal_not_navigable are reported as they happen. A capture with a failing check shows its reasons in red.
